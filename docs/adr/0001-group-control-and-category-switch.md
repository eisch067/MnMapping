# Layer group headings carry both a group control and a category switch

Layer group headings originally had one control, which hid the user's active subset and later restored it, and nothing turned every layer in a group on at once. The owner found that people usually want Parcels, Reference, Public lands, and each DNR Recreation heading either all on or all off. So those headings now also carry a category switch (All on / All off), separate from the group control. The two controls do different jobs, so neither replaces the other: the group control pauses and resumes your picks, and the category switch turns the whole category on or off.

## Consequences

- The category switch reads on only while every available layer in the group is on. Turning any one of those layers off by hand flips the switch to off.
- Turning the switch off turns every layer off and leaves nothing to restore. The heading says so, and points to the group control for hiding and restoring only your picks.
- Unchecking the group control turns the category switch off, because nothing is on. Turning the switch on while the group is suspended turns the whole group on.
- For Parcels, the switch follows the parcel range: counties within 50 miles of the centre of the screen turn on as they come into range and off as they leave it. Suspending and restoring the group pauses and resumes that following. Turning any county off by hand ends it, which leaves your remaining counties as a manual selection.
