# Unknown-county imagery audit

Search completed September 18, 2026, with manual viewer exports and a final targeted county-account audit incorporated through September 19. The initial pass covered the 25 counties whose research lead still lacked a confirmed current acquisition year or a verified current county service. The manual viewer reviews and final official-account search resolved four of the five statewide-only counties and 15 of the 17 older or recency-unverified counties.

Two independent searches were run for every county:

1. Public ArcGIS Online items, ArcGIS Enterprise REST catalogs, and the web-map definitions behind official county viewers.
2. Indexed official county pages, board packets, minutes, budgets, contracts, fee schedules, and reports for `aerial imagery`, `orthophoto`, `Pictometry`, `EagleView`, and `Nearmap`.

A year is reported only when it appears in a service, web-map layer, or county record. An ArcGIS item's modified date is not treated as an acquisition date. A vendor payment or multi-year agreement is evidence of access, but not evidence that a flight occurred that year.

## Outcome

- Directly displayable and added: Grant 2024/2021/2017, Lac qui Parle 2024/2020/2017, Lincoln 2026/2023/2020/2017, Marshall 2024/2020, McLeod 2026, Meeker 2024/2018, Stevens 2020, Wadena 2025, and Wilkin 2026.
- Newly dated but kept external: Freeborn 2020; Isanti spring/fall history through 2025; Kanabec 2024/2021/2018; Kittson 2024/2019; Lake of the Woods 2024/2021/2018; Martin 2026/2023/2020/2019; Meeker 2021; Murray 2024/2022/2019; Norman 2025/2022; Pine 2023/2018; Redwood 2026/2023/2020; Rock 2025/2022/2019; Stevens 2026/2023; Todd 2023; Waseca 2025/2021; Watonwan 2022; and Winona 2026/2022/2020. These sources use an official viewer without a reusable endpoint or licensed commercial delivery without an applicable embedding path.
- Partial local caches only: Freeborn 2020 Hollandale, Isanti 2020, and Watonwan 2022 local coverage. They are not represented as countywide layers.
- Remaining older or recency-unverified: Cottonwood and Roseau. The incorrect Lac qui Parle Beacon submission was not used; Lac qui Parle was instead resolved from its official ArcGIS owner account. Red Lake remains the only statewide-only county. Rice has no verified layer newer than 2023.

The unresolved group is now at the point where direct county GIS contact or a public-data request is the appropriate next step; repeating Beacon inspection or generic web searches is unlikely to establish the missing flight year or reuse permission.

## Recency-focused follow-up queue

For the research GUI, the stricter usability target is a verified non-statewide-NAIP source from approximately 2018 or newer. Under that definition, 84 counties meet the target and three need follow-up:

- **Statewide only (1):** Red Lake.
- **Older or recency unverified (2):** Cottonwood and Roseau.

Beacon, Pictometry, Nearmap, or other official viewers in the second group are treated as strong leads that may contain newer usable imagery. They are not treated as proof of an acquisition year until the layer metadata or county confirms it.
