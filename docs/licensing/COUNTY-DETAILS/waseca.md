# Waseca County

Not legal advice. Access date: 2026-09-18.

## Parcels — the best-supported statewide-only county in the audit

Statewide MnGeo Open Parcels aggregation, but Waseca is notably stronger than most statewide-only counties: the
county's own GIS page states *"Waseca County has adopted a formal policy establishing Free & Open sharing of
county GIS data,"* citing Resolution 2016-22 (passed 2016-06-21) — this is what feeds the MnGeo aggregation in
the first place. The resolution PDF itself is an image-only scan that could not be OCR'd this session, so its
exact operative language (including any commercial-use carve-out) is unconfirmed — only the county webpage's
paraphrase was verified. The county already publishes ESRI Shapefile/FGDB and Google Earth KML/KMZ downloads
via the Minnesota Geospatial Commons, affirmatively signaling that redistribution/downloading in those formats
is expected, not merely tolerated.

## Imagery

2025/2021 imagery is surfaced only via a Beacon/Schneider Corp viewer link — not embedded, link-out only.

## Business model notes

- Ads/free site: **YELLOW** — an affirmative open-data policy plus no found restriction, though the resolution
  text itself is unverified.
- Redistribution/offline: probably allowed given the Commons downloads already published in multiple formats.
- Fields to exclude: owner_name, own_add_l1, emv_total — standard caution despite the favorable posture, since
  the resolution's exact scope on ownership/tax attributes (vs. geometry) is unread.
