# Lake County

Not legal advice. Access date: 2026-09-18.

## Parcels — a self-qualifying "public domain" statement

Statewide MnGeo Open Parcels aggregation (47,116 records). Lake County's own GIS data page states: *"Map and/or
data is public domain in accordance with Section 466.03, subdivision 21 of Minnesota State Statute"* — but the
**same page** immediately narrows that: *"The data is restricted from use in property boundary establishment,
legal descriptions, navigation, or by third parties without authorization."* This is an explicit
authorization requirement for third-party use, not mere silence, and is the strongest county-specific
restriction found in the North batch. Lake County also "reserves authority to assess fees for any request that
may need additional staff time or use county resources."

## Imagery
