# Step 09 — Statewide Public-Land Boundaries

## Goal
Make public/recreational land obvious and trustworthy statewide.

## Priority statewide layers
- Wildlife Management Areas
- State Forest ownership/managed lands where authoritative
- State Parks and Recreation Areas
- Scientific and Natural Areas
- Aquatic Management Areas
- Federal public lands where a good statewide source exists

## Important rule
Do not imply that every acre inside an administrative boundary is public. Distinguish:
- administrative/unit boundary
- actual public ownership/managed parcel

## Requirements
- Clear legend and symbology.
- Clickable public-land polygons.
- Popup should include name, managing agency, and acreage where supplied.
- Link/source metadata should be retained.

## Acceptance criteria
- Public-land layers work beyond the initial five counties.
- Clicking a known WMA or other public unit returns useful identification.
- Administrative boundaries cannot easily be mistaken for ownership boundaries.
